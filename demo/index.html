Pick a corner of the map. The engine returns seven artists from the immediate sub-category, then widens — through curated cross-pillar bridges, then the rest of the pillar. Ask again and it keeps moving outward instead of repeating itself.

Throw a broken tag at it

The hard part isn't the taxonomy, it's that real files carry mangled artist strings. Every chip below is a shape that defeats a naive matcher — hover one to see what normally goes wrong. classifiedBy reports which step of the waterfall decided.
